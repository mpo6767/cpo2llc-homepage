### The mission

The U.S. now has the technology for people to represent themselves directly
on issues in Congress — not just vote every few years and hope, but let
representatives and senators know, issue by issue, how their constituents
actually stand.

This platform started as proof that the underlying technology works:
a secure, phone-based election system built for schools, using one-time
tokens instead of paper ballots. It's grown into something solid on its
own — and it's step one toward a larger constituent-voice platform where
reps and senators hear directly from the people they represent.

### What's under the hood

- **One-time token authentication** — no reusable credentials to leak or reuse
- **Tamper-evident, hashed vote records** — votes can't be silently altered
- **Real Maine-style instant-runoff (IRV) tabulation**
- **Rotation-proof credential handling**

### Why phone-based voting

Done right, phone-based voting can be faster and cheaper to administer than
mail-in paper ballots — with a stronger audit trail than most people assume
mail voting has.
